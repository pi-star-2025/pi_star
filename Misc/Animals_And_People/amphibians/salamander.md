## Salamander

Some compare the original goddess to salamanders, bc they sometimes like fires.

Salamanders are a feeling reptile, indicated by their stickiness and blinking habits. In addition, they have heat systems similar to lizards who have incredible heat tolerance. It's not healthy, but the combination of feeling and having heat immunity can make fires fun.

The original goddess lived in a real world of standing people, ending as a goddess of music. She wound up in a super blackhole, which told her your musical ability is your "first wish", your "first power". Your goal is to now find your second power. Her second power was developing the ability to burn alive forever, so as a goddess created a universe with many stars and is rumored to have pain tolerance high enough to "be" the earth in the very center.
